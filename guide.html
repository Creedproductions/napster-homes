How to use our Airbnb logbook — what every box means, in 3 minutes.

The logbook is our shared diary for the house. Whatever you type is saved instantly and everyone sees it live — phones, computers, everywhere. Every day must have something written on it, even if the house was empty.

1 · Logging in

1Open the logbook link.

2In the Team name box, type your own login name in small letters — mukama, ahereza or swimming.

3Tap Enter the logbook. Your phone remembers you — next time it opens straight into the calendar.

Ignore "Admin sign-in with Google" — that button is only for the boss.

2 · Reading the calendar

Each small square is one day. The colours tell the story at a glance:

  • ↓ 14:00 John — a guest checked in (arrow down, time, name).
  • ↑ 10:00 John — a guest checked out (arrow up, time they left).
  • Booked ✓ 50k — day is booked and the booking fee was paid (orange).
  • Booked · fee not paid — booked, but money not yet received (yellow).
  • red outline — a past day with nothing written. Someone must fill it.
  • M A S — small letter badges show who handled or recorded each thing.
  • ✏️ next to a date — that day's info was edited after it was first saved.
  • Green +120k / red −35k at the bottom of a day — money in and money spent that day.

Use the ‹ › arrows to move between months, and Today to jump back.

3 · Recording a day — tap the day, then use these boxes

First, tap the buttons that match what happened:

  • Check-in — a guest arrived today.
  • Check-out — a guest left today.
  • Guest staying — guest is sleeping over (no arrival or departure today).
  • Booked ahead — someone reserved this date for the future.

You can tap more than one — e.g. one guest leaves in the morning and another arrives in the evening.

Check-in boxes

  • Guest name — who arrived, e.g. John K.
  • Time entered — the time the guest came in.
  • Handled by — who received the guest (your name, or whoever did it).

Check-out boxes

  • Guest name — who left.
  • Time left — the time they went.
  • Handled by — who saw them off.

Booked ahead boxes

  • Guest name — who booked.
  • Booking fee paid — the money already received for the booking. Enter the amount → the day turns orange. Leave it 0 → the day stays yellow until the fee comes in.
  • Booked through — who took the booking.

"What happened this day" box

A short note about the house — "Cleaning done", "Bought gas", "House empty all day". If the day was quiet, this box is how you clear the red outline — never leave a past day blank.

"Money received" boxes

  • Amount — how much came in.
  • For — choose: Stay payment, Booking fee, Upgrade / extra, or Other.
  • Received by — who took the money.
  • Note — optional extra detail.

Then tap + Add money received. Add each payment separately.

"Money spent" boxes

  • Item — what was bought or paid for: cleaning, soap, tissue, gas…
  • Amount — the price.
  • Mobile money charge — the sending fee, if you paid by momo (put 0 if none).
  • Number sent to — the phone number the money went to. Always fill this — the weekly report shows how much went to each number.
  • Paid by — who spent the money.

Then tap + Add money spent. One entry per item is best.

Finish with Save day

Payments and expenses save the moment you add them; Save day saves the check-in/out, booking and note. If you tap ✕ next to an entry, it is deleted for everyone. If you change a day that was already saved, it gets an ✏️ mark showing it was edited and by whom.

4 · The panel on the right

  • This week — money in, money out and the balance for the current week, plus "Money sent to": every phone number money went to this week and how much.
  • Weekly totals — the same, week by week for the whole month.
  • Month grand total — money in, money out and profit for the month.

All of this fills itself from what you record — you never calculate anything.

5 · The Telegram bot

  • 8:00 PM — if nobody recorded anything that day, the bot reminds the group. Record early and it stays quiet.
  • 10:00 PM — the bot posts the day's summary, including a list of what each person put — so it shows if only one of us is doing the recording 😅.
  • Send /summary in the group anytime to see today's summary immediately.
The three rules: ① Record things the same day they happen. ② Never leave a red-outlined day — write a note even if it's just "house empty". ③ Always put the number you sent money to.
